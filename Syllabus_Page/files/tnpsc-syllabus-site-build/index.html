Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission · Combined Civil Services Examination — II

Group II & Group IIA
Syllabus & Exam Scheme

ஒருங்கிணைந்த குடிமைப் பணிகள் தேர்வு – II · தொகுதி II & IIA

Two services, one preliminary paper, and a Mains that splits on a single decision: Group II ends in an oral interview, Group IIA is settled entirely on the written score. Everything below is organised the way the commission scores it — by unit, by part, by mark.

The three stages

01

Preliminary Exam

Code 495 · Objective · Qualifying only

One common paper for both services — General Studies, Aptitude & Mental Ability, and a Language paper.

200 marks02

Tamil Eligibility Test

Mains Paper I · Descriptive · Qualifying only

Common to both services. Translation, summary, comprehension, letter and essay writing.

100 marks03

Mains Paper II

Scoring paper — decides the merit list

Group II: descriptive General Studies. Group IIA: objective General Studies + Reasoning.

300 marks

Where the two services diverge
