Exam pattern

Common qualifying paper for Group II and Group IIA Mains · Descriptive · SSLC Standard · 100 marks

  1. 1

    Translation (Mozhipeyarthal)

    (i) Tamil to English (ii) English to Tamil

  2. 2

    Summary Writing (Surukki Varaithal)

  3. 3

    Comprehension (Porul Unarthiran)

  4. 4

    Expansion from Notes / Outline (Surukkak Kurippilirundhu Virivaakkam Seithal)

  5. 5

    Essay themes drawn from Thirukkural

    • அSecular literature independent of caste and creed
    • ஆRelevance to everyday life
    • இImpact of Thirukkural on humanity
    • ஈThirukkural and eternal values — equality, humanism, and the like
    • உRelevance of Thirukkural to socio-political-economic events
    • ஊPhilosophical concepts in Thirukkural
  6. 6

    Letter Writing (official correspondence)

  7. 7

    Tamil Language Knowledge (Tamil Mozhi Arivu)

Syllabus themes

Topics the passages, letters, and essay questions draw from

  • Current affairs
  • Social issues
  • Environment-related themes
  • Indian economy-related themes
  • Science and technology
  • Art and culture
  • Rationalist movements — Dravidian Movement, Self-Respect Movement
  • Contemporary Tamil — computational Tamil, Tamil in courts of law, Tamil as an office language, new genres
  • Tamil Nadu's socio-economic progress and the Tamil Nadu Government's welfare schemes (women, farmers, etc.) — role of social reform movements in streamlining welfare schemes, reservation and its benefits, role of social justice and social unity in Tamil Nadu's socio-economic development
  • Writing complete sentences of one's own, identifying differences in meaning
  • Splitting words, antonyms, negative sentences, error correction
  • Essay writing on Thirukkural topics (list repeated from the exam pattern above)
