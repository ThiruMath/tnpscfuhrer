# Combined Technical Services Examination (Non-Interview Posts) 2026

The TNPSC Combined Technical Services Examination (CTS) is a major recruitment drive targeting 461 vacancies across multiple state engineering and technical services. 

---

## 📅 Key Dates & Details

- **Notification Date:** 20.05.2026
- **Last Date to Apply:** 25.06.2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Total Vacancies:** 461 posts
- **Exam Dates:** To be announced later by TNPSC

---

## 💼 Included Key Posts

This examination recruits for various technical positions, including but not limited to:
- **Assistant Geologist** (in Geology & Mining Department)
- **Assistant Engineer** (Agricultural Engineering)
- **Agriculture Officer** (Extension)
- **Drug Inspector**
- **Horticultural Officer**
- **Assistant Engineer** (various other branches)

---

## 📝 Scheme of Examination

The exam consists of two main papers:

### Paper I: Compulsory Common Paper
1. **Tamil Eligibility Test:** 100 questions (150 marks). Candidates must score a minimum of 40% (60 marks) to qualify.
2. **General Studies & Aptitude:** 100 questions (150 marks). Splits into:
   - General Studies (Degree Standard): 75 questions
   - Aptitude & Mental Ability (SSLC Standard): 25 questions
